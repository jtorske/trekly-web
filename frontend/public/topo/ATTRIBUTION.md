# Topographic backgrounds: data attribution

`mount-assiniboine.svg`: 50 m contours (every 5th line heavier) for
115.80–115.50° W, 50.80–50.94° N, around Mount Assiniboine.

Generated by `backend/scripts/make_topo_background.py` from the Canadian
Digital Elevation Model (CDEM), NTS tile 082J, Natural Resources Canada:
https://ftp.maps.canada.ca/pub/nrcan_rncan/elevation/cdem_mnec/082/cdem_dem_082J_tif.zip
